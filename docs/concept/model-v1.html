Dock for the Pixel 7a

  • Screen up, top end to the front. The app turns its screen by 180° so you can read it from the front.
  • The camera bar sticks out about 1–2 mm. The shelf gets a slot for it so the phone lies flat.
  • Make the pocket fit your case. Measure the phone with the case on, and add 1 mm.
  • Height: 110 mm from card to lens. At that distance the camera sees roughly 15 × 11 cm, and the small code on the card is about 40 pixels tall in a full photo. That should be enough to read. This is my estimate, and step 1 checks it.
  • The posts are a first guess at the height. Print them last, after step 1 shows how close the 7a can focus.
  • The phone weighs 193 g. That is why the shelf stands on four posts and doesn't hang on one arm.
